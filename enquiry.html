Frequently Asked Questions

Some common questions from our customers.

How far in advance should I order?

We recommend placing custom cake orders several days before the required date. This allows us enough time to prepare your order according to your requirements.

Can I customise my cake?

Yes. Customers can request different flavours, colours, sizes and designs. Please provide your requirements through the enquiry form.
