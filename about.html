About Amazingly Sweet Bakery

Freshly baked with care and passion.

Who We Are

Amazingly Sweet Bakery is a hypothetical family-owned bakery that has been serving delicious baked goods to the community since 2024 in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal.


Our passion for baking and commitment to quality has made us a destination for customers looking for sweet treats, fresh bread, pastries and custom-made cakes.

What We Offer

The bakery specialises in a wide range of freshly baked products. We take pride in using the finest ingredients and traditional baking techniques to create delectable treats that delight our customers.


  • Freshly baked breads
  • Cakes
  • Cupcakes
  • Pastries
  • Custom-made celebration cakes

Our History

How We Started

Founded in 2024, Amazingly Sweet Bakery is a hypothetical family-owned bakery based in Durban, KwaZulu-Natal.


Our journey is based on a passion for baking, dedication to quality and a love for creating exceptional baked goods.

Looking Ahead

As the bakery continues to grow, we remain committed to our core values of quality, creativity and customer satisfaction.


We aim to provide customers with delicious baked products and friendly, reliable service for many years to come. Every pastry, cake, and bread is crafted with care and attention to detail, ensuring that each bite is a memorable experience.

Our Values

The values that guide everything we do.

  • Quality: We are committed to using the finest ingredients and maintaining the highest standards in our products.
  • Creativity: We strive to create unique and innovative baked goods that exceed our customers' expectations.
  • Customer Satisfaction: Our customers are at the heart of everything we do, and we are dedicated to providing an exceptional experience.
  • Welcoming to All: We believe in creating a warm and inviting atmosphere for everyone who walks through our doors, no matter their background or preferences.

Our Mission

Our mission at Amazingly Sweet Bakery is to provide the community with the highest quality baked goods while creating a warm and welcoming environment for all.

Our Vision

Our vision at Amazingly Sweet Bakery is to be the most beloved bakery in the community, known for our exceptional quality and warm, inviting atmosphere.
